About Our School

Academic Excellence

Chitamba Primary School is dedicated to nurturing young minds through a well-rounded curriculum designed to foster critical thinking, creativity, and lifelong learning under the Ministry of Education.

Child Empowerment

We focus on dynamic educational strategies that build character, leadership, and practical skills, preparing our students to succeed and contribute meaningfully to society.

Safe & Supportive Environment

Our experienced teaching staff and welcoming campus ensure that every learner receives personalized attention and equal opportunity to excel academically and socially.

At Chitamba Primary School, we believe that every learner deserves access to meaningful education and a strong foundation for future success. Our school promotes a culture of excellence, inclusion, and continuous growth for both learners and staff.
